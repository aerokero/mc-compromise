# Progression

Description of the pack's current progression. This documents the current behavior, not the final simplification plan.

## General model

The pack leads the player through several parallel paths:

1. basic survival and cooking,
2. Copper and additional tools,
3. Iron → Carbon-Rich Iron → Steel,
4. Nether, Blaze Powder and sulfur/chemistry,
5. Diamond → Electrum,
6. Ancient City/Deep Dark → Echo Shards and Divine Star,
7. End → Dragon, Elytra and Netherite.

Progression is supported by extensive tutorial advancements and automatic recipe unlocking.

## Early Game

### First materials

- Wheat Seeds are displayed as Wheat Grain.
- Beetroot and Beetroot Seeds act as Tomatoes and Tomato Seeds.
- Rabbit Hide is Tattered Leather, while Leather is Sturdy Leather.
- Some spawn eggs act as ingredients: Enderman Egg as Flour, Shulker Egg as Dough and Magma Cube Egg as Flour Bag.
- Furnace is Basic Furnace, and Smoker is Oven.

### Tools and Copper

Copper has its own tool, armor, Shears, Compass, Mattock and Dolabra progression. Copper is currently separate from Redstone.

Bronze and Shakudo upgrade Copper Equipment at the smithing table:

- Bronze uses Phantom Membrane,
- Shakudo uses Shulker Shell.

### Food

An extensive cooking system is available early on: bread, soups, preserves, potato and carrot dishes, desserts and products made from processed mob drops.

## Iron, Steel and the Smithing Table

Iron is processed into Carbon-Rich Iron using a Coal Block. Carbon-Rich Iron is then smelted into Steel Alloy.

Steel upgrades Iron Equipment and adds:

- Steel Sword, Axe, Pickaxe, Shovel and Hoe,
- Steel Spear,
- Steel Shears,
- Steel Mattock and Dolabra,
- Steel Armor.

The pack also adds alternative steel recipes for several items normally made from iron, including buckets, bars, doors, trapdoors, chains and minecarts.

## Nether

### Materials

- Nether Quartz Ore has its vanilla name and behavior restored.
- Blaze Rod is restored as Blaze Rod.
- Blaze Powder is restored as Blaze Powder.
- Glowstone Dust is restored as Glowstone Dust.
- Glowstone is restored as Glowstone.
- Glistering Melon Slice is Nazar.

### Blaze Powder and chemistry

The pack contains potion, sulfur and chemical-substance systems. Blaze Powder is crafted from Blaze Rods and is no longer automatically consumed or generated as a custom mob drop.

### Netherite (formerly Adamant)

Vanilla Netherite currently has its vanilla display names restored: Netherite Scrap and Netherite Ingot. Some custom filenames still use `adamant`, but those names are not visible to the player.

Netherite is made from Netherite Scrap, Gold and Echo Shard. It is then used to create the highest-tier tools, weapons and armor.

## Deep Dark and Echo Shards

Echo Shards retain their vanilla uses and additionally take over the former Divine Fragment role.

They can be used for:

- Divine Star,
- Ender Eyes,
- Electrum Alloy,
- Netherite Ingot,
- Crystal Heart,
- Knowledge of Unbreaking.

Echo Shards are obtained, among other places, in Ancient Cities and other custom loot tables.

## End

The End is the main endgame stage, although the dimension itself has not been completely rebuilt.

Progression changes:

- Ender Dragon has its own advancement and reward,
- the first Dragon kill gives a Divine Star in the End at Y=100,
- obtaining Elytra has its own advancement,
- Bronze Elytra can be made at the smithing table,
- End Stone Bricks are renamed Brown Sandstone,
- Chorus Fruit has additional cooking uses,
- Ender Pearls have an additional Librarian/Mouthpiece trade.

Shulker is heavily changed: its loot table gives a Shulker Box instead of the standard Shulker Shell. Shulker Shell itself is a Shakudo Alloy material.

## Highest tiers

The simplified endgame path is:

```text
Copper
  ├─ Bronze
  └─ Shakudo

Iron
  └─ Carbon-Rich Iron → Steel

Diamond
  └─ Electrum

Netherite Scrap + Gold + Echo Shard
  └─ Netherite
```

## Related global systems

- The day/night cycle is about three times slower because the pack manually controls time.
- Sleeping adds a separate amount of time.
- Advancements unlock many recipes in stages.
- Villager trades are part of progression and provide materials, fish, books, maps and Divine items.
